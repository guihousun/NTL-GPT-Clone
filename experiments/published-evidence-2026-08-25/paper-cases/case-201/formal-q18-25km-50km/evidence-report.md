# Q18 Evidence Report — formal 25 km / 50 km support pair

## Accepted evidence

The NTL Engineer accepted a source-bounded USGS event context, an independently
decoded VNP46A2 observation package and a descriptive Analyst comparison for
two predeclared WGS84 geodesic supports.

| Support | Pre-event daily mean | First post-event mean | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 25 km | 1.482539 | 1.043513 | −29.61% |
| 50 km | 0.833820 | 0.874851 | +4.92% |

All values are in nW cm⁻² sr⁻¹. The pre-event window is 21–27 March 2025 UTC;
the post-event product is 28 March 2025 UTC, interpreted as the 29 March local
night in Asia/Yangon.

## Interpretation

The 25 km and 50 km first-night contrasts are retained as separate,
support-specific descriptive results. The evidence supports a transparent,
quality-qualified NTL workflow.

## Limits

This observational comparison does not establish earthquake causation, outage,
physical damage, recovery, or statistical significance. The small pre-event
samples, QA coverage, source-conflicted subsequent earthquake, and roughly
16-month gap to later observations remain material limits.
